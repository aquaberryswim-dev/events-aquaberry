Please read this before you sign up. When you tick the agreement box on an AquaBerry event sign-up form, you accept this agreement for that event.

### 1. Taking part is your choice

AquaBerry events can include exercise, such as a workout class, and time outdoors in the sun and heat. You choose to take part. You can rest, change an exercise or stop at any time.

### 2. The risks

Exercise and outdoor events carry risks, including muscle strains, sprains, falls, dehydration, heat illness, sunburn and insect bites, and, in rare cases, serious injury. You understand these risks, including risks not listed here, and you accept them.

### 3. You are ready to take part

You confirm that you are 18 or older and that you are physically able to take part. If you are unsure whether an activity is right for you, check with your doctor before the event. Tell the instructor before the class about anything that could affect you, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or anything unusual.

### 4. Release of liability

To the fullest extent the law allows, you release AquaBerry, its owners, the event host and property owner, instructors, sponsors, volunteers and helpers (together, "the event team") from any claims for injury, illness, loss or damage that come from taking part in or attending the event, including claims based on the ordinary negligence of the event team. You agree not to bring such claims. This release does not cover gross negligence or intentional misconduct.

### 5. Medical help

If you are hurt or unwell, you allow the event team to call emergency services or give basic first aid. Any medical costs are yours.

### 6. Your belongings

You are responsible for your own belongings. The event team is not responsible for anything lost, stolen or damaged.

### 7. Event rules and changes

Follow the directions of the instructor and the hosts. AquaBerry may change, move or cancel an event, for example because of weather, and will tell you by email.

### 8. Photos

Photos are handled separately. We only post photos of people who said yes on the sign-up form.

### 9. General

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida. If any part of it cannot be enforced, the rest still applies. Your sign-up records the date and the version of this agreement you accepted.
